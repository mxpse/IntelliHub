Resolution Hub

MockupAI brings verified team intelligence into the flow of conversation, helping global teams resolve recurring issues faster while preserving governance, multilingual clarity, and institutional knowledge.

Local Concept Prototype · Synthetic Data Only · Powered by MockupAI

Vision

Resolution Hub demonstrates how AI can preserve and surface team knowledge inside the collaboration environment the team already uses, with minimal behavior change. A governed AI advisor appears quietly within conversations — surfacing approved knowledge, historical precedent, and validated practices exactly when they're needed.

The goal: reduce resolution time from days to minutes while maintaining full governance, auditability, and human oversight of every recommendation made.

Purpose

Preserve Institutional Knowledge

Capture team insights and validated resolutions so they're available to every colleague, every time — regardless of region, language, or timezone.

Enforce Governance

Every recommendation carries a visible authority level — from approved policy, through validated playbook, to historical precedent. No color-only indicators; always text labels.

Multilingual Clarity

Full interface in English, French, Spanish, and Japanese. English remains the source of record. Translation confidence is displayed as illustrative, never as a safety guarantee.

Human Control

MockupAI never acts autonomously. Human review is required before any change, and contributors cannot self-approve best practices.

How It Works

Team member asks a question in channel
→

The intelligence panel shows recommendations alongside authority source, confidence score, escalation triggers, and supporting citations — giving team leads full visibility into why each step is suggested.

A contributor cannot self-approve a best practice. SME review is always required.

The prototype demonstrates a realistic international travel reimbursement configuration issue:

Scenario: Multi-day travel producing partial-day results

A consultant reports that a multi-day travel scenario is producing partial-day results when full-day results were expected. Team members respond with troubleshooting guidance: review travel-period classification, configuration hierarchy, rule precedence, interval definitions, supporting adjustment logic, and policy conditions.

MockupAI surfaces a governed recommendation backed by 14 comparable historical cases (12 resolved through validation of configuration precedence), an approved troubleshooting guide (KB-DEMO-204), and a validated team playbook (PLAY-DEMO-015).

What MockupAI shows

Recommended Action: 6 ordered steps from confirming travel-period classification through escalation to SME.

Similar Cases: 14 comparable cases with common pattern analysis, regional breakdown (EMEA, Americas, APAC), and illustrative resolution rate.

Knowledge: Approved guide, validated playbook, and one proposed best practice (pending SME review).

Team Context: Thread summary, suggested experts, and team roles involved.

Summarize & Identify

Summarize the thread, identify key investigation areas, and surface related historical CRM cases automatically.

Knowledge & Experts

Identify approved knowledge, validated practices, and suggest subject matter experts to involve.

Draft & Convert

Draft a response for human review. Convert the final resolution into a proposed knowledge entry (requires SME approval).

Multilingual Reasoning

Generate reasoning in the user's preferred language while preserving English as the source of record. CRM field names kept in English to avoid ambiguity.

Key Differentiators

Contextual, Not Intrusive

MockupAI appears as a quiet professional companion — helpful, grounded, easy to dismiss, never disruptive. It surfaces knowledge only when relevant discussion is detected.

Deterministic & Fixture-Driven

All interactions in the prototype are deterministic. No live AI calls, no external network requests, no authentication. Everything runs from synthetic fixture data.

Governance First

Visible text labels for Approved, Validated, Historical Evidence, and Unvalidated. Color is supplementary, never the sole indicator. Self-approval is structurally impossible.

Zero Infrastructure

Single Node.js process, no database, no build step. Deploy anywhere in seconds. When Sloan (AI pipeline) is available, reasoning is generated live; otherwise falls back to prepared text.
